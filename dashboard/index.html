    Methodology — how the tide is measured

    Every number on this page is derived from the sealed snapshot's own figures (TVL, 7d change, listing date, category, chains, token record). Nothing is hand-kept: no protocol list, no per-protocol text, no outside data.

    1. Velocity — the 7d TVL change, clamped to −40%…+60% and divided by 30, so one wild week cannot dominate. A +60% week scores 2.00; a −40% week scores −1.33.
    2. Trend persistence — a young protocol (listed under a year ago) growing fast is a breakout (+1.00); an old one growing fast is a confirmed trend (+0.40). A young protocol flat or falling is unproven (−0.80 young, −0.10 old).
    3. Category momentum — a multiplier from the category's own median 7d change in the snapshot: 1 + clamp(median ÷ 50, −0.40, +0.60). Heating categories lift their members; cooling ones drag them down.
    4. Core cap — (velocity + trend) × category multiplier is capped at 3.00, so no single week can run away with the board.
    5. Scale gate — log TVL, weak weight: 2.00 × clamp((log10 TVL − log10 $5M) ÷ (log10 $100M − log10 $5M), 0, 1). It keeps the board grounded in real money; a tiny base doubling from $5M does not beat a real $500M protocol growing 20%.
    6. Chain momentum — +0.08 per extra chain, up to +0.40. More chains means more places the tide can come in.
    7. No-token bonus — +0.80 when the snapshot records no token at all (no market cap, no CoinGecko id, no symbol). Those are the farmable set.

    Implied value maps the momentum score to dollars monotonically on a log scale, anchored on this board's own score range: the top row lands near $850M, the bottom near $2M. It is the radar's own claim about what the momentum is worth, never a market price.

    Farm plans are generated from each protocol's own fields — its category, its chains, its 7d change, its scale, its age, its watchlist status — by fixed rules. Momentum farming is time-boxed: enter while the tide rises, re-check weekly, and exit on two negative weeks.

    “No token recorded” means the snapshot records no token for the protocol. It is not proof that the protocol has none, and a token, if one ever comes, is not guaranteed.
